# AIAS Sales Script

## Open
“AIAS engagements come in three shapes: fixed build, build + empower, or managed partnership. The difference isn’t hours — it’s ownership and iteration.”

## Diagnostic Questions
1. Do you need one workflow or a repeatable system?
2. Do you want to operate it internally or co-manage?
3. Is your constraint speed or internal capacity?

## Routing
- Predictable delivery → Foundation
- Internal capability → Build + Empower
- Continuous optimization → Build + Manage + Scale

## Price Anchor
“We publish starting points. Once scope is locked, pricing is fixed.”

## Usage Explanation
“Each plan includes capacity. If usage grows, we upgrade your band.”

## Close
“With a short scoping call, I can confirm your tier and provide a fixed proposal.”
