# AIAS Ecosystem Positioning

AIAS is the authority and orchestration surface for a connected ecosystem that turns strategy into production delivery.

## Role Definitions

- **AIAS**: Advisory lead, systems design authority, governance owner, and public authority surface.
- **Reach**: Demand shaping, GTM qualification strategy, and growth intelligence inputs.
- **Zeo**: Implementation execution across automation, integration, and delivery engineering.
- **Settler**: Deployment operationalization, post-launch governance support, and handoff durability.

## Operating Relationship

```text
Client outcome goals
  -> AIAS strategy and governance design
    -> Reach demand + qualification intelligence
    -> Zeo implementation execution
    -> Settler operationalization and continuity
```

## Why This Model Exists

- Keeps decision authority and governance explicit.
- Separates strategy from execution without losing alignment.
- Improves reliability through deterministic handoffs.
- Reduces launch risk by requiring ownership at every layer.

## Community Entry Points

- Start with AIAS documentation and case studies.
- Use Reach and Zeo context to understand delivery pathways.
- Transition to engagement via strategy call once fit is confirmed.
