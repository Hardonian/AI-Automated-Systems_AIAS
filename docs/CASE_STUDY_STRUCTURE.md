# AIAS Case Study Structure

Use this structure for all public-facing case studies to keep proof clear and comparable.

## 1) Context

- Client type and operating environment.
- Starting constraints and business objective.
- Why automation was necessary now.

## 2) Challenge

- Process bottlenecks and risk points.
- Prior attempts and why they underperformed.
- Non-negotiable requirements (governance, timing, compliance, cost).

## 3) Approach

- Discovery findings that shaped scope.
- Deterministic boundaries and AI boundaries.
- Pilot design and acceptance criteria.

## 4) Implementation

- Delivery phases and timeline.
- Key workflow changes and control points.
- Handoff and enablement artifacts delivered.

## 5) Results

- Quantitative outcomes (time, conversion, quality, risk, cost).
- Qualitative outcomes (team confidence, governance readiness, decision speed).
- Stability indicators after rollout.

## 6) Governance and Continuity

- Ongoing review cadence.
- Ownership model after handoff.
- Expansion roadmap for adjacent workflows.

## 7) Evidence Checklist

Every case study should include:

- Baseline and post-implementation comparison.
- Explicit scope boundaries.
- Named operating constraints.
- Outcome metrics linked to business goals.
- Clear statement of what was not in scope.
