# AIAS Terms of Service (Repository Reference)

Last updated: 2026-02-18

## 1. Scope

AI Automated Systems (AIAS) provides consulting, advisory, automation architecture, and implementation services. Public website content is informational and does not by itself create a service contract.

## 2. Contract formation

Binding obligations, scope, milestones, pricing, SLAs, and acceptance criteria are defined only in executed agreements (for example, MSA/SOW/proposal).

## 3. Advisory boundary

Unless an executed contract explicitly defines deterministic acceptance obligations, AI-related outputs are advisory and require client validation before production use.

## 4. No universal guarantees

Any examples of performance improvement, speed, or savings are context-dependent and not guaranteed outcomes for every client.

## 5. Client responsibilities

Clients are responsible for:
- Providing accurate requirements and timely access.
- Validating outputs before high-impact use.
- Operating controls in their environment.

## 6. Security and compliance statements

AIAS describes security and governance practices, but does not claim third-party certification (for example SOC 2, ISO 27001) unless explicitly and verifiably stated in contract or official certification documents.

## 7. Intellectual property

Unless otherwise agreed in writing:
- Client owns deliverables specifically developed and paid for under the engagement.
- AIAS retains ownership of pre-existing methodologies, frameworks, templates, and generalized know-how.

## 8. Confidentiality

Both parties must protect confidential information and use it only for agreed engagement purposes.

## 9. Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, AIAS liability is limited to fees paid for the applicable engagement, except where non-waivable legal obligations apply.

## 10. Case studies and references

AIAS will not publish confidential client details in case studies without permission or lawful basis.
